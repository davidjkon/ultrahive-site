UltraHive Power-Ups: What Each One Does and Costs

Classic mode has five power-ups: Place, Clear, Slow, Flip, and Tiny. You buy them with coins. You earn one coin for every 100 points you score, and coins carry over between games. Coins cannot be bought with money.

Last updated November 15, 2026.

The five power-ups

Power-upCostWhat it doesBest used when
Slow60 coinsHalves the drop speed for 10 secondsThe speed has just stepped up and you need a few calm placements
Tiny80 coinsShrinks the current piece to a single hex, keeping its colorOne gap is blocking a row, or you need one more cell to finish a chain
Place100 coinsLets you tap any open spot to set the piece thereThe hole you need to fill is covered and cannot be reached by dropping
Flip120 coinsTurns the board upside downThe bottom of the stack is a mess and the top is clean
Clear150 coinsRemoves the lowest occupied rowThe stack is near the top and you need room right now

How coins work

  • Every 100 points scored earns one coin.
  • Coins are kept on your device and carry over from one game to the next.
  • There is nothing to buy. UltraHive has no in-app purchases.

Points scale with the level, so coins come faster the deeper you go. A single row cleared at level 10 scores 1,000 points, which is ten coins.

What to spend on first

Slow is the cheapest and the one to learn first. It buys time without changing the board. Tiny is the best value once you are chasing chains, because the shrunken hex keeps the piece's color. Save Clear for emergencies. It costs the most and does the least for your score.

Questions

What does Flip do in UltraHive?

Flip turns the board upside down. It costs 120 coins.

Slow, at 60 coins. It halves the drop speed for 10 seconds.
