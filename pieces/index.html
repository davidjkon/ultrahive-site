The 15 Hex Pieces in UltraHive

UltraHive has 15 pieces made of one to five hexagons: 1 monohex, 1 dihex, 2 trihexes, 6 tetrahexes, and 5 pentahexes. Each belongs to one of six colors, and every piece rotates in 60-degree steps.

What a polyhex is

A polyhex is a shape made by joining regular hexagons edge to edge. One hexagon is a monohex, two a dihex, three a trihex, four a tetrahex, and five a pentahex. They are the hexagonal counterpart of the square-celled shapes used in most falling-block games.

UltraHive does not use every possible polyhex. It uses 15 chosen to play well, with the tetrahexes as the core set and the pentahexes as the hard ones.

Why color matters

Color follows shape family, not size. All the straight bars are cyan, the compact shapes are yellow, hooks and L shapes are magenta, their mirror images are red, zigzags are orange, and the T is green.

A connected group of 10 or more cells of one color clears as a chain, even when no row is full. Because several shapes share each color, you can build a chain from different pieces. Cyan is the easiest to chain, since five of the 15 pieces are cyan. Green is the hardest, since only the T is green.

Rotation

Every piece has six orientations, one per 60-degree step. The piece turns in place around its center cell. Near a wall or the stack, the game nudges the piece sideways if that lets the rotation fit.

Pentahexes can split

From level 5, a five-cell piece has a one in five chance of breaking apart when it lands. The fragments settle separately. Plan a landing spot that still works if the piece does not stay whole.

Questions

How many pieces does UltraHive have?

Fifteen: 1 monohex, 1 dihex, 2 trihexes, 6 tetrahexes, and 5 pentahexes.

What is a tetrahex?

A shape made of four hexagons joined edge to edge. UltraHive uses six of them: bar, diamond, compact cluster, hook, mirror hook, and zigzag.

How many ways can a piece rotate in UltraHive?

Six. Pieces turn in 60-degree steps.

Which color is easiest to chain in UltraHive?

Cyan. Five of the 15 pieces are cyan: the dot and the four straight bars.
