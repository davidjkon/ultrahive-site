UltraHive

A $2.99 hexagonal falling-block puzzle from Ripsaw LLC. No ads, no in-app purchases, Family Sharing.

UltraHive drops honeycomb pieces onto an 11-column hex grid. Players rotate in 60-degree increments, clear rows, and trigger color chains. Classic mode has 25 intensity levels, five power-ups, a ghost piece, a hold piece, and Game Center leaderboards. Hive Bits is a 28-level puzzle mode with walls, moths, bee allies, and honey collection. The game is available on iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad, Mac with Apple Silicon (macOS 14+), and Apple Vision Pro. It does not collect personal data.
