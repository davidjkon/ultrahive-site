Privacy Policy

UltraHive Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the UltraHive game on iOS and Android, and to this website. UltraHive is published by Ripsaw LLC. Questions: support@ultrahive.app.

What we collect

UltraHive does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There is no UltraHive account. The game has no in-app purchases and no ads. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Scores and coins

Scores and coins stay on your device. They are not sent to Ripsaw LLC.

iOS

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, UltraHive uses Apple Game Center only for leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed into Game Center, scores stay local on your device.

Android

The Android app is offline. It does not use the INTERNET permission. It does not use Play Games. Scores stay on your device.

This website

ultrahive.app is a static marketing site. We do not run analytics, ads, or tracking on it, and we do not collect personal data through it. If you email support@ultrahive.app, we use that message only to respond.

Children

UltraHive is rated for all ages. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. The date at the top is the last revision.
