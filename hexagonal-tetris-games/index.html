Hextris

Hextris’s press kit says you rotate the hexagon so blocks falling toward an inner hexagon do not stack past an outer one. Connecting 3 or more blocks of the same color clears them. The same page says the game is completely free, open source under a GPL license, and for iOS, Android, and web. You can play it in the browser at hextris.io. The press kit does not state an ads policy. On November 2, 2026, the App Store id and Google Play package named in the game’s own page were not found, so this page does not link those store URLs.

Hex FRVR

The FRVR page for Hex says you drag block shapes into place and complete lines in any of three directions. The App Store listing is free and lists in-app purchases, including one named Remove Ads, for iPhone and iPad. The Google Play listing says the app contains ads and has in-app purchases, and that play does not need an internet connection.

Block! Hexa Puzzle

BitMango’s App Store listing says you drag blocks to fit them in the frame, blocks can’t be rotated, and there is no time limit. It says there are over 300 puzzles. It says the app contains banner, interstitial, video, and house ads, and that it is free to play with in-app purchases such as an ad-free option and hints. The listing is for iPhone and iPad. This page did not confirm a Google Play URL, so Android is not listed.

Hexable

MarketJS’s Hexable page describes an HTML5 game: drag and drop hexagon pieces onto empty slots, and create full lines to eliminate the tiles. That page does not state a player price or an ads policy. It does list a developer license at 1999 USD to deploy the game. That figure is not a price players pay.

Hexadu

Why a hex grid plays differently

On a hexagon grid, a turn is 60 degrees and each cell has six neighbors. A square falling-block game turns 90 degrees and usually clears horizontal rows. On a honeycomb, the same turn changes which sides touch, and a line can run in more than one direction. UltraHive uses that board: pieces rotate in 60-degree steps on an 11-column honeycomb. A full row clears, and so does a connected group of 8 or more cells of the same color. How UltraHive differs from Tetris.

UltraHive is our premium falling-block puzzle. Pieces drop on a neon 11-column honeycomb. You can hold one piece, and a ghost preview shows the landing spot. Color chains of 8 or more cells clear. Questions

Is there a hexagonal Tetris?

People use hexagonal Tetris for falling-block puzzles on hexagons. UltraHive is one of those, and it is our game. Pieces fall on an 11-column honeycomb and rotate in 60-degree steps. UltraHive is not affiliated with Tetris. Other games on this page use a rotating hexagon or drag-and-drop.

Is Hextris like Tetris?

Hextris’s own press kit calls it a puzzle game inspired by Tetris. You rotate a hexagon so blocks falling inward do not stack past an outer hexagon, and 3 or more touching blocks of the same color clear. UltraHive instead drops hex pieces onto a honeycomb. Hextris is not our game.

Which hex games on this page have no ads?

UltraHive has no ads. Hexadu’s App Store subtitle says no ads. Block! Hexa Puzzle’s App Store description says that app contains ads. Hex FRVR’s Google Play listing says that app contains ads, and its App Store listing includes a Remove Ads purchase. Hextris’s press kit and Hexable’s product page do not state an ads policy.
