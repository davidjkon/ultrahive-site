UltraHive Strategy: Scoring, Speed Levels and Tips

Your score in UltraHive comes from clears multiplied by your level. Clearing more at once, clearing on consecutive pieces, and clearing several color chains together all add bonuses. You go up a level every 10 lines, and pieces fall faster at each of the 25 levels.

Last updated November 15, 2026.

How scoring works

Each clear is measured in lines. A full row is one line. A color chain counts as one line for every 11 cells it removes, with a minimum of one.

After a piece touches down you have half a second to slide or rotate it before it locks. Each move resets that timer, up to 15 times per piece.

Tips

  • Hard drop everything you can. It pays twice what a soft drop does, and the points add up to coins.
  • Group colors early. A chain clears without a full row, so keep cyan with cyan and yellow with yellow while the pace is slow. The piece guide shows which shapes share a color.
  • Hold the long bar. A straight bar fills the deep gaps that nothing else reaches.
  • Read the queue. Three upcoming pieces are shown. Place the current one where the next one still has a home.
  • Use the lock delay. At high levels you cannot steer in the air. Drop near the target and slide the piece in during the half second before it locks.
  • Expect splits from level 5. Five-cell pieces sometimes break apart on landing. Avoid dropping them across a gap you cannot afford to fill.
  • Keep the level in mind. Every clear is multiplied by your level, so a clear you set up at level 3 is worth more if you cash it at level 4.

Questions

How do you score more points in UltraHive?

Clear more lines at once, clear on consecutive pieces to build a combo, and reach higher levels. Every clear is multiplied by your current level.

How do you level up in UltraHive?

You advance one level for every 10 lines cleared. Classic mode has 25 levels.

How fast is level 25 in UltraHive?

A piece falls one step every hundredth of a second. Level 1 is one second per step.

What is a perfect clear worth?

1,000 points multiplied by your level, for emptying the whole board.
