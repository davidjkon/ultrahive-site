No Wifi Games: What Works Offline and How to Check

A no wifi game is one that runs with no internet connection at all: no wifi and no mobile data. The reliable way to know is to test it in airplane mode before you need it. UltraHive plays offline on iPhone and Android.

Last updated November 23, 2026.

Why some games need a connection

  • Ads. Free games load ads from the internet. Some will not start a level until an ad loads.
  • Accounts and saves. Games that keep your progress on a server need to reach it.
  • Multiplayer and live events. Anything played against other people needs a connection.
  • Downloads after install. Some games fetch levels or art the first time you open them.

Single-player puzzle games that are paid for up front are the most likely to work with no connection, because none of the four applies.

How to test a game before a flight

  1. Install the game and open it once while you are online, so any first-run download finishes.
  2. Close the game completely.
  3. Turn on airplane mode. Leave wifi off.
  4. Open the game and play for a few minutes. Try each mode you care about.
  5. Close and reopen it, still offline, and check that your progress is there.

If all five steps pass, the game will work on a plane, on the subway, or anywhere else without signal.

Questions

What is a no wifi game?

A game that runs with no internet connection, meaning no wifi and no mobile data. Single-player games that are paid for up front are the most likely to qualify.

How do I know if a game works without wifi?

Open it once online, close it, turn on airplane mode, and play. If it starts, plays, and keeps your progress after a restart, it works offline.
