The rules in one minute

  • Pieces made of one to five hexagons fall onto an 11-column honeycomb.
  • Each piece turns in 60-degree steps, so it has six orientations.
  • Fill a row to clear it. Connect 10 or more cells of one color and that chain clears too.
  • You go up a level every 10 lines, and pieces fall faster.

Questions

Can I play UltraHive online for free?

Yes. The demo on this page runs in a browser and covers Classic mode levels 1 to 3. Nothing is saved between visits.

Is there a hexagonal falling-block game I can play in a browser?

Yes. The UltraHive demo is a falling-block puzzle on an 11-column honeycomb where pieces rotate in 60-degree steps.

Does the demo work on a phone?

Yes. It supports touch: swipe to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop faster, and swipe up to hold.
