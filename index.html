UltraHive

Honeycomb Hexagon Fun

A fresh take on the falling-block puzzle genre, built on a hexagonal grid that changes everything you know about stacking and clearing.

iPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple Vision Pro

Features

Everything you need for hex-based puzzle mastery

An 11-column honeycomb board, strategic power-ups, and chain combos that reward skill and planning.

Hexagonal Grid

Increasing difficulty with escalating drop speeds. Master the early stages and push your skills to the limit.

How to Play

Simple to learn, deep to master

1

Rotate & Position

Tap to rotate hex pieces and swipe to position them across the 11-column grid.

2

Complete Rows

Fill entire rows to clear them from the board and score points. Chain multiple clears for bigger rewards.

3

Build Color Chains

Connect 16+ same-colored cells for massive bonus clears. Plan your colors carefully.

4

Use Power-Ups

Spend earned coins on 5 strategic abilities. Place Anywhere, Row Clear, Slow Time, Gravity Flip, or Shrink.
