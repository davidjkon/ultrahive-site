How to Play

How to play

Rotate in 60° steps, clear a row, or connect 8 or more cells of the same color. Full rules.

1

Rotate & Position

Tap to rotate in 60° steps and swipe to move across the 11-column honeycomb. A ghost preview shows the landing spot, and you can hold one piece.

2

Complete Rows

Fill an entire row to clear it. Classic mode has 25 intensity levels.

3

Build Color Chains

Connect 8 or more cells of the same color to clear that chain.

4

Use Power-Ups

Five power-ups: place anywhere, clear a row, slow time, flip gravity, or shrink to a single hex.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UltraHive?

UltraHive is a premium hexagonal falling-block puzzle by Ripsaw LLC. Pieces drop onto a neon 11-column honeycomb and rotate in 60-degree steps. It costs $2.99 once, with no ads and no in-app purchases, on iPhone and Android.

Is UltraHive a hexagonal Tetris?

Yes. It is a tetris-like falling-block puzzle on hexagons instead of squares. You can hold one piece, and a ghost preview shows where the piece will land. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How much does UltraHive cost?

UltraHive is $2.99 one-time on the App Store and Google Play. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Family Sharing is available on Apple.

How do you play UltraHive?

Drop pieces on the 11-column honeycomb, rotate them in 60-degree steps, and use the ghost preview and hold piece to plan a landing. Clear a full row, or connect 8 or more cells of the same color. Classic mode has 25 intensity levels and five power-ups: place anywhere, clear a row, slow time, flip gravity, and shrink to a single hex. Full rules are at https://ultrahive.app/how-to-play/.

What is Hive Bits?

Hive Bits is a puzzle mode with 28 handcrafted levels. The boards include walls, moths, bee allies, and honey to collect.

What devices does UltraHive support?

UltraHive runs on iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad (iPadOS 17+), Mac with Apple Silicon (macOS 14+), Apple Vision Pro (visionOS 1.0+), and Android. The iOS app launched on February 24, 2026 (version 1.6.0 on July 23, 2026). Android has been on Google Play since September 10, 2026.

Does UltraHive work offline?

The Android app is fully offline and does not use Play Games. On iOS, Apple Game Center is used for leaderboards only. Scores and coins stay on the device.

Does UltraHive collect personal data?

No. UltraHive does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, and it does not use analytics or tracking tools. The privacy policy is at https://ultrahive.app/privacy/.
