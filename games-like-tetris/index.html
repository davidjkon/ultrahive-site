For Tetris fans

A game like Tetris, built on hexagons

UltraHive is a falling-block puzzle for people who love Tetris. Pieces drop, you rotate and place them, and full rows clear. The difference: the board is a honeycomb, so every piece is made of hexagons and turns six ways instead of four. It's $2.99 on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, and Android, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What feels familiar

  • Drop, rotate, clear. Pieces fall from the top, and filling a row clears it.
  • Ghost piece. See exactly where the current piece will land.
  • Hold piece. Save a piece for later and swap it back when the board needs it.
  • Rising speed. 25 levels, from 1 second per drop down to 10 milliseconds.
  • Leaderboards. Global Game Center leaderboards on Apple devices.

What's new for Tetris players

  • Six-way rotation. Hex pieces turn in 60° steps, so each piece has six orientations to think about.
  • 15 hex pieces. Monohex dots, dihex bars, trihex triangles, tetrahex hooks, and pentahex snakes, each in one of six colors.
  • Color chains. Connect enough same-colored cells anywhere on the board and they clear too, setting off cascades.
  • Power-ups. Spend coins on PLACE, CLEAR, SLOW, FLIP (invert gravity), and TINY (shrink to one hex).
  • Hive Bits. A separate mode of 28 handcrafted puzzles with walls, moths, bee allies, and honey to collect.

Questions from Tetris players

Is there a Tetris game with hexagons?

Yes. UltraHive is a falling-block puzzle played on an 11-column hexagonal grid instead of a square one. Pieces fall, you rotate and place them, and full rows clear, just like Tetris, but every piece is made of hexagons and rotates in 60° steps. It's $2.99 on the App Store and Google Play.

What is a good game like Tetris for iPhone and Android?

If you want the Tetris feel with something new to learn, try UltraHive. It keeps the core loop of dropping, rotating, and clearing rows, and adds a hexagon grid, same-color chain clears, five power-ups, and a 28-level puzzle mode called Hive Bits. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, and Android, costs $2.99, and has no ads or in-app purchases.

Is UltraHive a Tetris clone?

No. UltraHive is inspired by falling-block puzzles, but it plays differently. The honeycomb board means pieces can face six directions instead of four. The 15 pieces range from single hexes to five-hex pentahexes. Clearing same-colored groups is a second way to score. And UltraHive is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Tetris Company.

Does UltraHive have a hold piece and ghost piece like modern Tetris?

Yes. UltraHive has a hold piece that lets you save a piece and swap it back later, and a ghost piece that shows exactly where the current piece will land.

How hard is UltraHive for a Tetris player to learn?

Most Tetris players pick it up in a few games because the controls and goal are familiar. The part that takes practice is hex rotation: a piece has six orientations, and rows on a honeycomb board interlock differently from square rows. Classic mode starts at 1 second per drop and ramps to 10 milliseconds per drop at level 25.

Is UltraHive free?

No. UltraHive is a one-time $2.99 purchase with no ads and no in-app purchases. On the App Store it supports Family Sharing for up to 6 family members.
