Games Like Tetris: The Kinds of Falling-Block Puzzle

A game plays like Tetris when pieces fall one at a time, you rotate and place them, and filled lines clear. Games in the genre differ mainly in three things: the shape of the grid, what causes a clear, and how the game is paid for. UltraHive is a hexagonal one, $2.99 once with no ads.

Last updated November 15, 2026.

What the games have in common

  • Pieces made of a few connected cells fall into a well.
  • You move and rotate each piece before it locks.
  • Completing a line removes it and scores points.
  • Pieces fall faster as you go, until the stack reaches the top.

What a hexagonal grid changes

On squares, a piece has four orientations and a cell touches four others. On hexagons, a piece has six orientations and a cell touches six others. That has three effects.

  • More ways to fit. Six rotations mean a piece that looks wrong often fits after one more turn.
  • Rows are staggered. Neighboring columns sit half a cell apart, so a flat row is a zigzag, and gaps hide in different places.
  • More piece shapes. UltraHive uses 15 pieces of one to five cells. The piece guide lists them.
