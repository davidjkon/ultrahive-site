Privacy

UltraHive does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There is no UltraHive account. The game has no in-app purchases and no ads. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Scores and coins stay on the device. On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, Game Center is used only for leaderboards and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The Android app does not use the INTERNET permission and does not use Play Games. This website does not run analytics, ads, or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
